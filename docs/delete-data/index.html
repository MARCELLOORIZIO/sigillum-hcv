Data Deletion

How to request deletion or correction of SIGILLUM data.

How to request deletion

Send a request to the temporary support contact:

marcelloorizio@yahoo.it

Include:

  • Your name and contact email.
  • The HCV-ID or certificate reference, if available.
  • A short description of the data you want deleted or corrected.
  • Whether the request concerns app data, Registry data, technical identity data or future KYC data.

What can be deleted

Data stored only on your device can be deleted by removing it from the app, device storage or Photos library. Server-side personal data may be deleted where technically and legally possible.

Registry integrity

Some Registry records may need to remain available to preserve certificate integrity, anti-fraud evidence and auditability. In that case, SIGILLUM may remove or minimize personal data while retaining technical certificate records needed for verification.

KYC provider data

If KYC is enabled in the future, identity documents and biometric checks should be handled by the selected KYC provider. Deletion requests may need to be processed both by SIGILLUM and by that provider according to their policies and applicable law.

Italiano

Per chiedere cancellazione o correzione dati, invia una richiesta con HCV-ID, email di contatto e descrizione dei dati interessati. Alcuni dati tecnici del Registry potrebbero dover restare disponibili per preservare integrita, verifica e tracciabilita del certificato.
