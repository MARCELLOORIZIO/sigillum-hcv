Privacy Policy — service summary

Updated 5 October 2026

English

SIGILLUM processes account and security data, Creator/device technical identity, subscription status, identity-verification status, HCV-IDs, signed certificates, hashes, capture metadata and content required for the operation requested. Photos/videos and HCVPACK are protected in the app; before external release of media, an encrypted original reference may be stored in private cloud infrastructure. Stripe Identity is used for Creator identity verification. Apple handles App Store payments. Render hosts application/Registry services and Cloudflare R2 is used for encrypted technical references. SIGILLUM does not sell personal data or use user content for behavioural advertising.
