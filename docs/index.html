You create. SIGILLUM protects the origin.

Create. Certify. Share. Verify.

Photos and videos originate in the SIGILLUM Camera. Text is written and certified inside the app. Before a photo or video leaves SIGILLUM, a protected original reference is registered so later copies can be checked against what existed before distribution.

You

Capture, record or write. Then save, share or verify.

SIGILLUM

Creates the HCV-ID and signed certificate, links Creator and device, protects the origin and registers the reference.

Verify later

Check an exact original, a social copy, an authorized derivation or published text. When available, compare media directly with the protected reference.
