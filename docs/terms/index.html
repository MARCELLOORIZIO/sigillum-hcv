Terms of Service

Last updated: 3 July 2026

SIGILLUM provides technical tools to create, sign and verify digital content through HCV-ID, certificates, fingerprints and an online Registry.

Scope of the service

SIGILLUM may help users create verifiable technical evidence for photos, videos, text and related packages. Verification can show whether content is linked to a SIGILLUM certificate, whether the file matches the certified fingerprint, whether a compatible derivative is detected, and whether screen-replay or other caution signals are present.

No absolute truth guarantee

SIGILLUM does not prove the absolute truth of a scene and does not replace a legal, notarial or forensic expert report. A risk warning requires caution and is not automatic proof of falsification. A successful verification indicates that the available technical checks are consistent with the certificate and Registry data.

User responsibility

Users are responsible for the content they create, import, verify, publish or share. Users must not use SIGILLUM to impersonate others, falsify identity, violate third-party rights, distribute unlawful content, mislead others or interfere with the service.

Identity

Until a formal KYC process is enabled, creator identity may include technical device identity and a user-declared name. A declared name is not the same as a legally verified identity. If KYC is enabled, the app should distinguish technical identity from verified legal identity.

Registry and availability

Online verification requires access to the Registry. The Registry may be temporarily unavailable because of maintenance, network problems or hosting limitations. Offline packages may allow verification of some data without immediate Registry access.
