Terms of Service — service summary

Updated 5 October 2026

English

SIGILLUM creates a technical chain for a verifiable origin. Photos/videos certified as originals originate through the SIGILLUM Camera; text is written and certified in the app. HCV-ID, hashes, signed certificates, Creator/device binding and Registry data support later verification. Before external release through supported flows, photos/videos receive a protected original reference. A valid Registry record alone is not a media-integrity verdict. Screen/scene indicators are technical evidence and do not by themselves prove the substantive truth or falsity of a scene.
