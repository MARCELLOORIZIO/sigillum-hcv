Support

Create, share and verify with a few actions.

English

Create: use the SIGILLUM Camera for photos/videos, or write text inside SIGILLUM and certify it. Share: save/share normally after SIGILLUM has registered the required protected media reference. Verify media: share or select the received file in SIGILLUM. Verify text: copy the published text including its SIGILLUM/HCV-ID line and paste it into Text verification. Results distinguish identical original, compatible copy, authorized derivation, modified content or inconclusive verification.
